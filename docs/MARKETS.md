# International Markets, Currency & Compliance Specification (docs/MARKETS.md)

KUMAR sells to six primary global luxury markets: **European Union, United Kingdom, United States, Japan, UAE (Dubai), and India**.

---

## 1. Regional Specifications
| Region | Currency | Pricing Display | Default Size System | Measurement Unit | Tax / Customs Policy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **European Union** | EUR (€) | Tax-inclusive (VAT) | EU (44–60) | Centimeters (cm) | Standard EU VAT; DDP shipping |
| **United Kingdom** | GBP (£) | Tax-inclusive (VAT) | UK (34–50) | Centimeters (cm) | UK VAT; DDP shipping |
| **United States** | USD ($) | Tax added at checkout | US (34–50) | Inches (in) | Duty calculated upfront; DDP shipping |
| **Japan** | JPY (¥) | Tax-inclusive (whole yen) | JP (1–5) | Centimeters (cm) | Consumption tax inclusive |
| **UAE (Dubai)** | AED | Tax-inclusive (5% VAT) | EU / UK | Centimeters (cm) | UAE VAT; DDP shipping |
| **India** | INR (₹) | Tax-inclusive (GST) | Standard EU/UK | Centimeters (cm) | GST invoicing; Razorpay integration |

---

## 2. Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) Guarantee
The checkout engine will calculate all landed costs and customs duties up front, ensuring no customer ever receives a surprise courier invoice at delivery.
